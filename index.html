Frequently asked questions

Which rings and trackers can you review?

Ōura and WHOOP. Using something else? Email us at hello@osleeve.com before booking and we’ll let you know if we can work with it.

How do I send you my data?

It depends on your device. Both involve asking for a copy of your data, which can take a little while to arrive, so request it as soon as you book.

Ōura: Log in to the Membership Hub, select Export data, then Request your data. Ōura can take up to 10 days to prepare it and will email you when it’s ready.

WHOOP: Request your export by following WHOOP’s guide. It usually arrives within 24 hours.

Once you have the file, upload it through the form in your confirmation email.

How quickly will I get my answers?

Within 7 days of receiving your data.

Because Ōura exports can take up to 10 days to arrive, Ōura users should allow two to three weeks from booking to call.

WHOOP users can usually have their call within a week or so.

What happens to my data?

We use it only for your review, store it securely, and delete it within 90 days unless you ask us to keep it. We never share or sell it.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is a sleep and recovery data review to help you understand patterns in your data. It is not a medical diagnosis or treatment.

Is this right for me?

It’s for people who wear an Ōura or WHOOP, have at least 30 days of data, and feel tired despite getting enough time in bed.

Please speak with your GP first if you snore loudly, someone has noticed you stop breathing while asleep, or you regularly fall asleep during the day. Those symptoms need proper medical assessment, not a data review.

Why are places limited?

Because we personally review every set of data. We only take on the number of reviews the two of us can read properly in a month.

What if my score doesn’t improve?

Sleep responds to lots of things, and some changes take longer than two weeks to show. If your scores haven’t moved by the check-in, we’ll look at why and adjust your plan at no extra cost.

Refunds apply if we can’t find anything worth changing in your data, and you can ask for one any time up to the end of your call.
