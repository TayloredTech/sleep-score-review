Caffeine

What caffeine do you have, and what time is your last one?

Tick all that apply, and add the time.

Misunderstood: caffeine stays in your system for hours, so an afternoon coffee is still active at bedtime. Pre-workouts and some teas are hidden sources. “I can drink coffee and still fall asleep” doesn’t mean it isn’t affecting deep sleep.

SECTION 6

Alcohol

How many alcoholic drinks do you have a week, and when?

The most misunderstood one: it helps you fall asleep, but it raises night-time heart rate, lowers HRV and breaks up sleep later in the night. It’s often the clearest signal in ring data.

Your data and consent

Upload your data export *

Pick your device in section 1 and the export steps for it will appear here.

How to export your Ōura data

  1. Sign in to Ōura on the web with your Ōura account.
  2. Choose Trends in the left-hand menu, then set the date range at the top.
  3. Download the CSV.
Ōura’s own export guide

Use as long a date range as you have. Three months is ideal, one month is the minimum worth reading. A full Ōura data request can take up to 10 days to arrive, so start it now if you are going that route.

How to export your WHOOP data

  1. Open the WHOOP app and tap the menu icon at the bottom right.
  2. Scroll down, choose Data Export, and pick your date range.
  3. Tap Export. WHOOP emails you a link to the CSV files.
WHOOP’s own export guide

Most exports arrive within 24 hours and the download link expires after 7 days, so save the file as soon as it lands. Use as long a date range as you have. Three months is ideal.
