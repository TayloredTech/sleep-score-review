1. Which one do you wear?

Export from Whoop

  1. In the Whoop app, tap More. On iPhone go to App Settings, then Data Export. On Android it's Data Export straight from More.
  2. Check your email address and tap Create Export.
  3. Whoop emails you a download link, usually within 24 hours. Download the file and upload it below. The link stops working after 7 days.

Whoop's own guide

Export from Ōura

  1. Log in to the Ōura Membership Hub.
  2. Choose Export data, then Request your data.
  3. Ōura emails you when it's ready. Go back to the Hub, download it and upload it below.

It can take a few days to arrive, so request it now. Ōura's own guide

Export from Apple Health (Apple Watch)

  1. On your iPhone, open the Health app.
  2. Tap your picture or initials at the top right.
  3. Tap Export All Health Data, then Export. Save the file (export.zip) to Files and upload it below.

This file is often bigger than 50MB. If it is, just reply to our email and we'll send you another way to share it. Apple's own guide

Export from Garmin

  1. Sign in to your Garmin account at garmin.com.
  2. Go to Data Management, then Export Your Data, and tap Request Data Export.
  3. Garmin emails you a download link, usually within a couple of days. Upload the zip below.

If the file is bigger than 50MB, reply to our email and we'll find another way.

Ultrahuman ring

Ultrahuman doesn't offer a direct export of ring data. On an iPhone, switch on Apple Health sync in the Ultrahuman app, then use the Apple Watch steps above. On Android, reply to our email and we'll work it out with you.
